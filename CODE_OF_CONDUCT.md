# Code of conduct

Second Wall Street follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be welcoming, patient and constructive. Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing or sexualised content.
- No promotion of scams, market manipulation or misleading financial claims.
- Maintainers may remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules.

Report problems privately with GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or through the private [security form](https://github.com/AICreditWallet/second-wall-street/security/advisories/new) if it involves someone's safety or data. Reports are handled confidentially.
